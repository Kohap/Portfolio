# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS (single-file `index.html` + `404.html`), served from a GitHub Pages repo root at `https://kohap.github.io/Portfolio/`. No framework or build step.

## Users

Protocol teams and foundations hiring an independent Web3 security researcher. They land here to answer one question fast: is this someone I can trust with an audit, based on evidence and method rather than claims.

## Product Purpose

Demonstrate Gift's Web3 security research capability — EVM and Solana contract/program review, dApp and wallet-flow surface testing, threat modeling, fork-based PoCs, and developer-ready reporting — so that teams convert from reader to contact. Success is a credible inquiry or commissioning decision.

## Positioning

Evidence-forward research: every capability is tied to a public artifact (ErrorLens, AgentPay Guardian, Web3 Audit Toolkit, contest record) rather than to credentials or slogans. The identity is deliberate — dark green and lime, terminal/mono voice, technical and exact.

## Operating Context

Auditors, developers, and hiring teams evaluate the page on desktop and mobile, frequently under time pressure, often cross-referencing the linked GitHub/contests. Platforms named: Sherlock (Metric & Tare, Jul 2026), SolanaBR skill bounty (PR #35), GitHub (`/Kohap`).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- EVM & Solana security review; dApp/API surface testing; wallet-flow review; threat modeling; vulnerability research with fork-only PoCs; developer-ready reporting.
- Constraint: contest submission repositories are private and are listed only as a record without external links; three private disclosures are recorded without publishing exploit detail.
- Constraint: authorized testing only; production testing never assumed; active exploit paths, private reports, and sensitive protocol details are not published before a responsible-disclosure process completes.
- Undecided: the interactive sample finding wording is illustrative placeholder; false data must not be introduced.

## Brand Commitments

- Name "Gift" with the "GIFT." logotype (period in lime) is locked.
- Lime-on-dark identity is locked: dark green background (`#0d120f` scale), `#c5f246` lime accent, paper/ink light surfaces only inside the sample-finding block.
- Terminal/mono voice is core: JetBrains Mono for labels, code, and data; Bricolage Grotesque display; Schibsted Grotesk body (selected 2026-08, detector-clean).
- Display face and accent palette are committed; do not swap them back to Inter/Space Grotesk.

## Product Principles

- Evidence outranks claims: each capability maps to a public artifact or a recorded, verifiable result.
- Method matters visually: threat modeling, validation, and verification are shown as a repeatable process, not a highlight reel.
- Privacy is a product feature: private disclosures and contest submission repos stay private; make the policy explicit, never leak detail.
- Motion is functional: terminal-style animation and scan motifs exist to reinforce the technical identity, not decoration; reduced-motion is respected.
- One page, one clear action: every section routes toward contacting Gift for an authorized review.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

prefers-reduced-motion supported site-wide (intro, ticker, typing effect, reveals disabled; sweep paused/static). Focus-visible outlines, aria-labels on icons and the security signal, and contrast built around the dark theme. No WCAG-level conformance standard was confirmed.
