Overview

This site exists to present security work, capabilities, and a way to initiate contact. It does not sell data, run advertising, or build profiles of visitors.

What I collect

In ordinary use, this site stores:

  • Hosting access logs (IP address, browser type, requested pages), held by the hosting provider, not by me.

If you send an email or message through the listed channels, you voluntarily provide whatever content you include in that message. Only what is needed to respond is kept.

How it is used

Non-identifying and access-level data is used only to keep the site up, detect abuse, and understand that content is reachable. Messages you send are used solely to reply to the inquiry.

Sharing

No personal data is sold, rented, or traded. Data is shared only where legally required or to protect against abuse of the site.

Cookies & analytics

This site does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party ad analytics. Where enabled, the hosting provider's aggregate web analytics (page views, referrers, general geography) is used to confirm the site is reachable and understand that content is used; it does not build profiles or track individuals. Links to Cal.com for booking are operated by Cal.com and have their own policy. The contact form composes an email on your device; nothing you type is stored by this site.

Security

Published findings are redacted or withheld until disclosure terms permit. Evidence for private research is never published here.

Your rights

You can ask to see or remove data you have sent through the contact channels at any time. Where the law grants additional rights, they are honored.

Contact

For questions or requests about this policy, reach out via the contact options on the main page.
